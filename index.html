What the experiments establish

Compact models close much of the frontier gap

The paper separates the strongest single run, seed robustness, and transfer to an external benchmark. Keeping those results distinct avoids overstating any one number.

01

Fine-tuning works

The strongest Llama-3.1-8B run improves from 29.22% to 42.57%. Across the evaluated models, AutoGraphQL supervision yields gains of up to 22.57 percentage points.

02

Near-frontier overall

The best 8B single run reaches 42.57% versus Claude Sonnet 4.5 at 44.23%. Across three seeds, Llama averages 42.92 ± 2.04%, a 1.31-point mean gap.

03

The gains transfer

On the external benchmark of Kesarwani et al. (2024), the fine-tuned open-weight models reach 49.31%, 50.31%, and 51.20%.

The adapted Llama model also exceeds the Claude reference on directives, filters, pagination, and mutations. These are public-benchmark results; the 2,100-instance hidden split remains withheld for future evaluation.

More about the benchmark
Why this benchmark exists

Text-to-SQL has Spider and BIRD. GraphQL has a different problem.

Enterprise GraphQL APIs are schema-specific: each organization exposes its own types, fields, arguments, filters, and mutations. A model that works on one API may fail on the next. AutoGraphQL is designed around that reality: bring a schema, generate validated data for it, adapt a model, and evaluate whether the model generalizes to schemas it did not see in training.

Each instance is a validated triple — (schema, natural-language query, ground-truth GraphQL query) — synthesized through schema-aware query generation, then checked for both syntactic validity and successful execution before it's admitted to the corpus.

  • Cross-schema, not fixed-schema. The 104 training, 49 public-benchmark, and 20 hidden-benchmark schema sets are pairwise disjoint.
  • Construct-level diagnosis. Toggle the result table's category breakdown to see aliases, directives, fragments, filters, pagination, and mutations individually.
  • Execution-validated ground truth. Every reference query is schema- and execution-checked, then deduplicated by canonical structure.
  • A genuinely withheld benchmark. 2,100 instances across 20 schemas are not released and are reserved for future evaluation.
